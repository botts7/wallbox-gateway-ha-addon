Charging window optional

Restrict charging to cheap hours (e.g. midnight–6am) so it never runs when power is expensive.

The window bounds the charge: it starts just-in-time to finish by the end of the window and stops at the window end. The window can cross midnight (e.g. 23:00 → 07:00). To let a departure deadline push charging outside the cheap window, turn on “keep charging past the window” (overrun) and/or “start before the window” (pre-start) above — otherwise the window wins and the car may not reach target if the cheap hours are too short.
